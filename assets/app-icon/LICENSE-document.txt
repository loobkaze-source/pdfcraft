Unigreen PDF document icon (pdfcraft-document.svg and pdfcraft-document.ico).

The Unigreen three-leaf mark on a Unigreen dark tile with the app label, made by Unigreen Power
Co., Ltd. for Unigreen PDF, its distribution of PdfCraft. It replaces upstream PdfCraft's
document icon and contains no third-party material.
